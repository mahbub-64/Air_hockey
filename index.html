HOW TO PLAY

PLAYER 1 (Bottom Half)

  • 📱 Mobile / Tablet: Touch and drag anywhere on the bottom half of the table.
  • 🖱️ Desktop Mouse: Move cursor inside the bottom half. Striker follows cursor seamlessly.
  • ⌨️ Desktop Keyboard: Use W A S D keys to move with high-speed arcade acceleration.

PLAYER 2 / AI (Top Half)

  • 📱 Mobile / Tablet: Touch and drag anywhere on the top half of the table simultaneously!
  • ⌨️ Desktop Keyboard: Use ▲ ▼ ◄ ► Arrow keys.
  • 🤖 Solo Practice: Switch to VS AI mode in the top menu to play against the computer.

RULES & TIPS

  • Neither striker can cross the center line. Defend your half!
  • Hit the puck while moving forward to deliver a high-velocity power smash!
  • Bank shots off the side rails and rounded corners to bypass your opponent's striker.
  • First player to score 7 goals wins the match!
